💡 What is FairRank?

Prestige via Faculty Flow

Traditional commercial university rankings rely heavily on subjective reputation surveys, endowment wealth, and self-reported metrics. FairRank maps the PhD-to-faculty academic placement network to uncover real-world institutional prestige from unbiased open data. By consolidating records across Wikidata, OpenAlex, and ORCID, FairRank runs a Weighted PageRank algorithm where hiring a university's doctoral graduates acts as an endorsement of that institution. The result is an objective academic placement-prestige index.

📖 Metric Explanations & Methodology

🏆

FairRank Score

Calculated via Weighted PageRank (scaled by ×1,000 for clarity). It measures the probability of landing on a university when following observed faculty hiring paths. Universities that place PhD graduates into top research institutions receive compounding prestige scores.

🎓

External Placed

The total count of verified PhD graduates from this university who currently serve as professors/faculty at other institutions in the global network. Institutional self-hires are excluded to evaluate true outward market influence.

📈

Δ (Prestige Shift)

The difference between a university's FairRank placement position and its published QS World Ranking (QS Rank − FairRank). A positive green value indicates the university's academic hiring power exceeds its general survey reputation.

🌐

Hiring Connections

The total number of directed hiring ties between institutions in the network (11,500+ edges). Each edge represents one or more verified faculty members holding a doctorate from the alma mater and employed by the hiring university.

⚙️

Damping Factor (0.85)

Standard PageRank parameter ($\alpha=0.85$). 85% of prestige propagation is channeled through actual placement links, while 15% is uniformly distributed across the academic graph to guarantee convergence and prevent dead ends.

🛡️

Zero Subjective Bias

Unlike traditional tables influenced by peer surveys, university marketing budgets, or arbitrary faculty-student ratios, FairRank is computed purely from empirical placement graphs derived from open bibliographic and academic records.

Data Sources: OpenAlex API · ORCID · Wikidata SPARQL · Benchmark: QS World Rankings
